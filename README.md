## 📌 About the Project

This is the official portfolio website for **[3R Waste Management](https://3rwastemanagementt.netlify.app/#about)**, a Pune-based environmental engineering firm established in 2006. 

The website showcases the company's turnkey services, engineering capabilities, and project portfolio across four core verticals:
- **Water & Wastewater Treatment:** Engineering Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP), Sewage Treatment Plants (STP), and Water Treatment Plants (WTP).
- **Solid Waste Management:** Solutions for organic waste composting (OWC), anaerobic biogas, and bio-fuel pellets.
- **Operations & Maintenance:** Comprehensive annual O&M contracts for environmental infrastructure.
- **Consultancy Services:** EIA reporting and government consent/subsidy compliance (MPCB/CPCB/MoEF).

### Key Features
- **Modern & Responsive UI:** Designed for seamless navigation across desktop and mobile devices.
- **Project Showcase:** Highlights flagship projects and corporate clients (including Taj Group, Hyatt, JW Marriott, and HSBC).
- **Interactive Lead Capture:** Integrated proposal request and direct contact forms.
